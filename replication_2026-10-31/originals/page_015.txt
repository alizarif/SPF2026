Taken together, these facts highlight a long-lived but evolving panel of macroeconomic experts
with persistent heterogeneity in training, roles, and institutional context. In the next section,
we use these observable differences as inputs to construct synthetic forecasters that mirror the
human SPF participants.
4
Simulating the SPF with LLMs
We next outline our simulation procedure. We use the information described in the previous
section to construct personalized profiles that mirror human participants and generate synthetic
forecaster personas. To closely match the forecasting environment humans face, we embed each
AI forecaster in a prompt that includes real-time macroeconomic data and historical SPF median
forecasts, and have them generate point forecasts for each quarter between 1999Q1 and 2023Q4.
In addition, we extend the simulation to include an out-of-sample test covering 2024Q1 through
2024Q4, allowing us to evaluate model performance beyond its training window.
