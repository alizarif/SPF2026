Simulating the SPF With an AR(1) Model
This appendix shows forecast results generated by a simple AR(1) model on median human
forecasts. Specifically, letting ¯ht denote the median human forecast of any variable in the SPF,
the AR(1) model takes the following form:
¯ht = α + ρ¯ht−1 + εt,
(D.13)
where εt is independent and identically distributed according to a normal distribution with
mean zero and a constant variance. The coefficients are estimated using an expanding window,
such that predictions for the survey at time t are based on information up until time t − 1.
Table D.15 reports mean absolute errors of AI-generated SPF forecasts and forecasts implied
by an AR(1) model on median human SPF forecasts, relative to the mean absolute errors
of human SPF forecasts. The statistical significance of the difference between AI and AR(1)
forecasts is evaluated using the Diebold and Mariano test (Diebold and Mariano (1995), Diebold
et al. (1999)). The results show that human forecasters do not rely solely on AR(1) models for
generating SPF forecasts, as the relative mean absolute error of AR(1) forecasts are generally
different from one. In addition, the results show that AI forecasts add value beyond the AR(1)
model, as the AI and AR(1) model are statistically different and the relative mean absolute error
is lower for AI forecasts compared with AR(1) forecasts, almost everywhere.
