We select GPT-4o mini as our primary model based on a series of preliminary experiments
evaluating performance and stability. Using CPI inflation as a test case, we compared GPT-3.5,
GPT-4, and GPT-4o under multiple temperature settings: 0 for deterministic outputs, 1.0 as
the default balanced setting, and values above 1.5 to introduce greater variability.12 Given its
strong performance and its architectural similarity to GPT-4o, GPT-4o mini offers an efficient and
reliable option for large-scale simulations. Our implementation uses an AI agent built with the
OpenAI Assistants API to manage tasks such as generating forecasts, handling query–response
chains, and performing additional data processing.13 Ultimately, however, this framework is
model-agnostic. Our implementation uses an automated pipeline to generate quarter-specific
queries and to parse responses into a standardized format consistent with the SPF.
4.2
Prompt Design
Our objective in the prompt design is to elicit forecasts from LLMs that are directly comparable
to those submitted by human SPF participants. The baseline prompt, described in Prompt 1,
assigns the model the role of an SPF panel member making a forecast at a specific survey date.
Each AI forecaster is instructed to report numerical forecasts for the current quarter (t) and the
next four quarters (t + 1 to t + 4) in a standardized format, and to provide a brief 1–2 sentence
explanation of their predictions.14 The prompt explicitly emphasizes that the forecaster must
base their judgment only on information available at that point in time and must not use data
or events from the future. In this way, we aim to simulate a professional forecaster who uses
