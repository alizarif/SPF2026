Prompt 2: Prompt for a generic forecaster
This prompt is used to generate AI forecasts for a generic forecaster, i.e., without including forecaster characteristics.
The input variables are highlighted in brackets and blue text and consist of real-time data, previous forecasts, survey
dates, and forecasting variable names.
You are a professional economic forecaster participating in the Survey of Professional Forecasters.
You have expertise in macroeconomic analysis and forecasting. Use your professional judgment and the
data provided to make your forecasts.
We are in [date_q]. You are about to fill out the forecast form for [date_q]. Using only the
information available to you as of [release_date], please provide your best numeric forecasts for:
[variable_definitions].
For quarters: t (current), t+1, t+2, t+3, t+4, and annual averages for this year and next year.
Real-time data available (from [prev_quarter]): [real_time_data].
Previous SPF panel forecasts ([prev_quarter], for t, t+1, t+2, t+3, t+4): [variable_forecasts_text].
Do not incorporate any data beyond [release_date]. Consider all relevant economic conditions and
Federal Reserve actions up to [release_date].
Format your response as: [variable_name]: t, t+1, t+2, t+3, t+4, this year’s avg, next year’s avg
Provide only numerical values.
