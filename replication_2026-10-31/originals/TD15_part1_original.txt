implied by an AR(1) model of human SPF forecasts relative to the mean absolute errors of human SPF forecasts.

ahead, respectively. For each forecasting horizon, the table boldfaces the lowest MAE relative value between the AI
and AR(1) model. The P-val column reports p-values and significance levels for Diebold and Mariano 1999 tests of
the statistical significance of the difference between AI and AR(1) forecasts. Significance levels: ⋆ p-value≤0.10, ⋆⋆
