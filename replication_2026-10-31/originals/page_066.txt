Reasoning Classification Details
C.1
Keyword Lists
Each LLM-generated reasoning response is classified using keyword-frequency rules. A re-
sponse is tagged with a category if it contains at least one keyword from that category’s list:
• Mechanical: “trend,” “historical,” “average,” “mean reversion,” “pattern,” “previous,”
“past,” “typical,” “normal,” “baseline,” “extrapolat.”
• Policy: “fed,” “federal reserve,” “monetary policy,” “fiscal,” “stimulus,” “rate hike,” “rate
cut,” “tightening,” “easing,” “central bank,” “government,” “policy response,” “interven-
tion.”
• Model: “phillips curve,” “okun,” “gdp growth,” “economic model,” “structural,” “re-
lationship between,” “economic theory,” “multiplier,” “elasticity,” “economic indicator,”
“leading indicator.”
• Event-driven: “covid,” “pandemic,” “crisis,” “recession,” “shock,” “war,” “collapse,”
“lockdown,” “unprecedented,” “disruption,” “financial crisis,” “lehman,” “subprime,”
“outbreak,” “emergency.”
C.2
ECB-Style Aggregation
Following the ECB Survey of Professional Forecasters framework:
Model-based = Model ∪ Mechanical
Judgment-based = Policy ∪ Event
C.3
Crisis Period Definitions
• Financial Crisis: 2008 Q3 – 2009 Q4
• COVID-19 Pandemic: 2020 Q1 – 2020 Q4
• Inflation Shock: 2022 Q1 – 2022 Q4
All other quarters are classified as “Normal” periods.
