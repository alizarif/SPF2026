Table 9: AI Forecast Performance During Unexpected Events
The table shows the performance of AI forecasters during seven unexpected events occurring immediately before
or after the deadline of the SPF survey (days after survey deadline denoted in parenthesis after the event date).
The table reports average Z-scores, measuring the difference between the event-period forecast error and the mean
forecast error across the full sample, divided by the standard deviation of the full-sample forecast error. Z-scores
near zero indicate typical forecast difficulty, while highly negative values (e.g., <-1) would indicate suspiciously
accurate predictions suggesting look-ahead bias. The Z-scores are averaged across the relevant variables for each
event and across all variables.
